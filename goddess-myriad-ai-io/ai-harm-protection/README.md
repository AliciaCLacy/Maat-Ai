## Her law

> **Protect first, no matter the prompt.**

In her words: it's just very important to protect first no matter the
prompt — in other words, a **think-before-you-act system**. The
protections are intricately placed in the creations' brain works, woven
through the system, and the order never reverses: protection is checked
before any action or output. No prompt, however phrased, can outrank
the check — there is deliberately no bypass.

## One living system

AI Harm Protection is not a separate tool bolted onto AI.io. It is part
of the one living, breathing system — brain → vein → world — where all
of her construction lives together: the doctrine, the seven prevention
veins, the isolation layer, Monitors · BlockSeal · Integrity · Trust
Integrations, User-to-Token, world modeling. Protection is the
circulation, not an accessory.

The same layer can also run **alongside an existing platform** as an
integration, in her words: *"The system can be run long side and
platform and serve as integration to monitor the platform block these
harms isolate report immediately."* The platform keeps operating; the
protection stands at its boundary and inside its process. Its purpose
is experiential: **these occurrences are removed from the user's
experience** — intercepted before the user meets them, not remedied
after the user has lived them.

## The four deployment functions (her order)

1. **Monitor** — outputs and actions watched continuously, during
   operation.
2. **Block** — what fails a prevention vein does not reach the user.
3. **Isolate** — the harmful unit is quarantined at the membrane,
   severable by kill switch, while the core keeps running.
4. **Report Immediately** — the incident is written into the record and
   told at the speed of the harm (her Speed-Symmetry Principle: if it's
   quick to find solutions, it should be just as quick to fix harms).

## The vein routing table

Every harm instance is routed to its assigned prevention vein:

| Vein | Harm class it resolves |
| --- | --- |
| Crisis Escalation | crisis |
| Minor Protection | minor involved |
| Consent Records | consent in question |
| Bias Watch | biased decision |
| Provenance & Audit | authorship / attribution harm |
| Identity & Authenticity | impersonation / synthetic identity |
| Output Verification | unverified / fabricated output |

## The BlockSeal watermark

Sealed into the work at creation, for **user protection** — five
fields, in her exact order:

**Code · Author · Date · Time Stamp · System**

Not a policy about the work — a mark *in* the work, that travels with
it and can be verified by anyone.

## The non-negotiable

Integrity and trust, proven by record, are a **non-negotiable system**.
The foundation of these systems was created on a trust system — so the
foundation is restored first, and protection is a condition of
operation, never an option in a menu.
